# TASK11 P1-C2.2 — Adaptive multi-view evidence selection review

2026-10-17. Implementation is opt-in and offline-tested. Stop here for review; no P1-C2.3, P1-C3, P1-C4, live Azure, external document processing or production integration was activated.

The selector retains the protected original-view unit and the protected recovered alternate-view unit in different PDFs. Source eligibility improves from one unit under either individually selected representation to two unique source units across representations. This is a narrow engineering result. It does not establish general clinical PDF adaptability or useful validated clinical chunk throughput.

## Contracts and boundaries

`src/multiview.py` adds three boundaries: `build_multiview_selection`, `validate_multiview_selection`, and `selected_units`. `MultiViewSelection` records one verified PDF SHA-256, original plan identity, separate alternate evidence/plan, deterministic selection identity, request budget, explicit unit decisions and incomplete-document status. `UnitSelection` records view, unit identity, outcome, native ownership, related representations and reasons. These are internal sidecar fields, never clinical content fields.

Original evidence and its original `ClinicalPlan` remain attached to `SourceDocument`. Optional `multiview_selection` retains the alternate graph and plan. Both graphs have the same PDF/configuration/page/native-block identity. Every original buffer, mapping and warning must remain unchanged in the alternate graph. This deliberately bounded implementation stores two graph representations; it shares PDF identity, not a deduplicated persistent buffer registry. The resource cost is measured below. No canonical text buffers are concatenated across views.

For each owned concept occurrence and required context occurrence, selection recomputes the existing native support proof. Missing certificates remain unresolved; a larger eligible/text count cannot waive them. Native block spans identify source ownership, separately from canonical view spans. Identical native ownership, literal text and clinical context selects the original once and records the repeated representation. Disjoint ownership permits complementary units. Overlapping interpretations with different ownership/context are held. Conflict propagation occurs before duplicate suppression, so an earlier duplicate cannot hide a later conflict.

Uniquely aligned literal native propositions with contradictory numeric values, explicit operators or units receive `conflicting_evidence`; alignment uses the existing lexical quantity-context helper and both retained native extraction families. Repeated/ambiguous contexts do not establish an association. This is bounded lexical detection, not semantic adjudication. Other association problems retain existing planner blocks.

All unit outcomes are explicit: supported original, supported alternate, supported both, duplicate representation, conflicting evidence, unresolved source association, extraction repair, or source review. Source warnings retain their classifications, locations and original resolution status. Alternate-only warnings are included in coverage. Multi-view coverage remains incomplete and cannot assert human approval.

## Source provenance and protected anchors

Native proof equality is checked against independently built single-view plans in the anchor tests. Validation packets omit internal labels/hashes from clinical evidence. A packet-local matching span is explicitly labeled `request_evidence_span`; canonical/native ownership remains separate in `selected_source`, avoiding a fabricated canonical document coordinate.

## Adversarial and compatibility evidence

Tests cover identical paragraphs in multiple views, complementary units within one mixed-layout PDF, localized alternate-plan damage, overlapping ownership/context interpretations, conflicting native values/signs/units, repeated text in different native contexts, cross-page/multi-block material, table dependencies, unknown/unlocalized visuals, image-only pages, rotated/overlapping text, critical unlocalized warnings, unverified PDF identity, altered certificates/inventory/warnings, tampered batches/selections and unknown chunk origins. Positive recovery and false acceptance are exercised separately.

The eight unsafe-source condition cases and four explicit quantity-conflict cases admit zero selected units (0/12). Additional schedule/storage tampering cases reject. These are software fixture labels, not estimates of clinical sensitivity or specificity. The repeated-text fixture exposes an existing repeated-margin/section-association limitation: both occurrences remain held, rather than being merged based on their words. Cross-page and multi-block statements also remain held by the current certificate contract.

New synthetic mixed-layout, continued-page, repeated-context and separate clinical-context PDFs supplement the earlier synthetic families. These are independently constructed engineering fixtures. No new licensed external clinical document collection or independently clinician-adjudicated held-out benchmark was obtained; generalization remains unproven.

Historical compatibility was checked on 180 actual source maps: 90 P1-A.1 artifacts (legacy expanded/compressed/benchmark copies), 30 P1-B1, 30 P1-C1 and 30 P1-C2. All loaded with empty selection metadata. The older P1-A directory contains no `source-*.json` artifacts and is not counted as a verified source-map family. New compressed/expanded read-back rejects altered selection/certificate metadata. No historical artifact was migrated or rewritten.

Installed Python 3.13.7 and Pydantic 2.13.5 were checked locally, including the `model_copy` signature, against the official [Pydantic model documentation](https://docs.pydantic.dev/latest/concepts/models/). No library or deployment capability was added or assumed.

## Clinical ingestion limits and review boundary

There are no real-source validator PASS or REVIEW_REQUIRED drafts from this benchmark: both faithful source quotations are FAILED by unchanged length policy. Clinically accepted chunks, clinical approvals and human review corrections are zero/not measured. Unsupported concepts incorrectly admitted across the unadjudicated clinical corpus and supported concepts incorrectly blocked remain unknown. Local fixture results cannot establish clinical error rates.

Selection is deliberately conservative: certificates require one local native block and sufficient independent corroboration. Native extractors may share font-map errors; literal quantity matching does not prove clinical arm/endpoint/context semantics. Overlapping partial unit partitions are held rather than automatically repackaged. Full source completeness is intentionally unavailable in this opt-in increment. Two-view graph duplication and repeated integrity validation impose meaningful storage/CPU/memory costs; automatic activation requires further resource and independent-source review.

P1-C2.3 remains planning only: evaluate localized native repair first, retain explicit page/region provenance and uncertainty, keep table/figure relationships held, and keep OCR/visual routes unavailable until typed capability, permissions, data handling, cost and accuracy are separately verified. No such external route was enabled. Coherent evidence packaging/length policy, durable unit recovery, live Azure quality benchmarking and production RAG remain separate unimplemented milestones.

**P1-C2.2 is the review checkpoint. No downstream implementation or clinical/production approval is inferred.**
