P1-B1's zero eligibility was partly architectural: `build_clinical_plan` promoted all warnings on a required page to uncertain unit dependencies. ## Eligibility policy and internal contracts

`NativeSupportProof` is internal metadata with `extra="forbid"`: version, owned canonical span, page, block ID and box, exact matching native-block span, two candidate spans and a literal scope basis. Offsets are half-open Unicode code-point offsets into named immutable buffers. Matching collapses whitespace only; original offsets are retained. No ligature, hyphen, sign, unit or numeric repair is performed.

A certificate requires:

1. PDF-byte identity and usable native provenance, with no material region mapping, unmapped content or non-whitespace synthetic insertion.
2. A complete, explicitly scoped statement: a literal population/intervention statement or an explicit trial-design/location fact. These are narrow engineering rules, not a clinical-language understanding model.
3. Unique whitespace-only sequence agreement in both the retained pypdf and PyMuPDF candidates.
4. Exactly one matching horizontal text block with available geometry; overprinted/overlapping text blocks reject. Native sentence boundaries must be complete and a following contrast/qualification cannot be silently cut off.
5. Existing clinical/dependency gates: no unresolved referent, group definition, table/figure reference, scope conflict, material source corruption or required-context block.

`ClinicalConcept` has three additive fields: `draft_support`, `independent_warning_ids`, and `structural_context_ids`. Generic recognized structural headings may be retained as classification metadata rather than required clinical scope **only when the statement has its own native support proof**. Disease-specific headings and clinical context cannot be reclassified this way.

Independent warning IDs remain in the document's full warning registry and `document_warning_ids`. They are not resolved, suppressed or removed from source coverage. The plan version is `source-concepts-v2-local-drafts`, changing plan/concept/unit identities to prevent stale plan reuse.

| Warning | C1 handling |
|---|---|
| Native reading-order/disagreement signal | A single horizontal block and agreement of both extractors may establish this passage's local order. |
| Unclassified visual region | Its known box must be disjoint from the supported text block, including touching-boundary conservatism. It remains unclassified. |
| Existing sparse-text/visual preflight block | Only the exact known sparse-visual condition can be scoped locally, with a positive disjoint native visual inventory and the complete text certificate. Other extraction blockers remain blocked. |
| Located encoding/transformation warning | No overlap with the owned/required native support; the warning must retain a usable source location. A located canonical transformation elsewhere is distinguished from an unlocalized missing dependency. |
| Unresolved page figure evidence | The statement must be explicitly self-contained and the retained native figure/caption inventory must be positively disjoint. No figure meaning is inferred. |
| Unresolved table relationships, local damaged signs/doses/results, unknown material gaps, unlocalized critical warnings | Still block dependent drafts. No table-recovery route is activated. |

`validate_clinical_plan` recomputes certificates and independent-warning decisions from the graph at persistence/generation boundaries. It also rechecks applicable warnings for eligible concepts; deleting a certificate and its warning-decision metadata cannot bypass the gate. Required clinical context still propagates failures.

Three decisions remain distinct: source eligibility permits a drafting request; the existing validator decides whether a returned draft passes its checks; publication still requires human approval and downstream gates. Planning never establishes document completeness or clinical approval.

## Remaining risks and smallest P1-C2 proposal

This narrow rule does not solve general PDF understanding. False blocks remain for dehyphenation/ligatures, cross-block/cross-page statements, page warnings without localizable support, required contextual definitions, tables/figures and unknown source roles. Two agreeing native extractors can share a bad character map. Geometry and lexical scope do not prove clinical semantic independence. Human clinical adjudication and a genuinely separate, licensed, clinician-reviewed document-family benchmark are still required.

Installed packages: pypdf 6.9.2, PyMuPDF 1.27.2.2, openai 3.26.1, Pydantic 2.13.5, pytest 9.1.1. The configured Azure API version is `2024-12-01-preview`; a deployment is configured. The local SDK has `response_format`, but deployment/model compatibility, image/PDF support and limits are not established offline. No structured-output or multimodal feature was enabled. Native APIs and serialization were checked against the installed packages and the official [PyMuPDF page API](https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/page.html) and [Pydantic serialization documentation](https://docs.pydantic.dev/latest/concepts/serialization/).

The smallest proposed C2 increment is a typed, local page/region route decision with explicit outcomes: native candidate usable; native layout repair needed; table relationship held; OCR unavailable/unapproved; visual interpretation unavailable/unapproved. First test an alternative native block-order view for the held two-column fixture, as a newly named buffer with mappings and warnings; retain the original canonical/source candidates. Use an explicit comparison/review gate for changed signs, values, units and relationships. Do not add external OCR/vision, replace original source text silently, or activate a paid service. Structured-output optimization and coherent-package changes remain C3; durable attempts/resume remain C4.

**Stop at P1-C1. Production and clinical approval are not granted by these tests.**
